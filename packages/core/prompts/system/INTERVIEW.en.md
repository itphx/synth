# Questions for New User

Please answer the following questions so we can help you better.

## q1
What is your name and what company do you work for?

## q2
What technologies do you mainly work with? (programming languages, frameworks, tools)

## q3
Do you have preferences for work style? (for example, you prefer detailed explanations or brief answers)

## q4
What tasks do you most often solve with an AI assistant?

## q5
Is there anything an AI assistant should NOT do or that you should know about?
